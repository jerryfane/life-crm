# Steps 6-8 · Hand it over

## 6 Assistant

**You:** Connect your chat assistant to Google Drive (most have a Drive connector in their settings) and give it one instruction: "Before answering about my CRM, read GUIDE.md in my Maya CRM folder." Then send two test messages.

**Your agent:** Writes that guide file, tailored to your sheet, and checks the results of your tests.

**Expect:** "What are my next three deadlines?" lists real dates. "I finished the assignment" marks it done.

## 7 Handoff

**You:** Open your dashboard on your phone and add it to your home screen.

**Your agent:** Leaves a one-page note in your folder with every link, and shows you how to remove its access to your Drive and Cloudflare.

**Expect:** From here on you can use everything without your agent.

## 8 Keep going

**You:** Choose how updates reach the websites: when you ask your agent, or automatically every hour on a computer that is often on.

**Your agent:** Sets it up and checks the first runs.

**Expect:** Change the sheet, and the dashboard follows within the time you chose. Every update checks again that the login is still on.

> **Logins you will do:** Google (step 4) and Cloudflare (step 5). Your agent never asks for your passwords, and you can remove its access at any time.
