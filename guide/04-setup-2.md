# Setting it up: steps 5-8

## 5 Publish

**You:** Create a free Cloudflare account and turn on the login with its help (about 10 minutes the first time). Read your public page line by line.

**Your agent:** Shows you everything on your computer first. Puts your data online only after the login works, and checks that a stranger sees only a login page.

**Expect:** First a "locked" page with no data, then your dashboard. You sign in with your email and a one-time code.

## 6 Assistant

**You:** Connect your everyday chat assistant to Google Drive, add one instruction, and send two test messages.

**Your agent:** Writes a guide file in your folder: which tab is which, what the assistant may change, what needs your yes.

**Expect:** "What are my next deadlines?" lists real dates; "I finished X" marks it done.

## 7 Handoff

**You:** Open the dashboard on your phone and add it to your home screen.

**Your agent:** Leaves a one-page note with every link, and shows you how to remove its access.

**Expect:** From now on you can use everything without your agent.

## 8 Keep going

**You:** Choose how updates run: when you ask, or every hour.

**Your agent:** Sets it up and checks the first runs.

**Expect:** Change the sheet, and the websites follow within the time you chose.

> **Logins you will do:** Google (step 4) and Cloudflare (step 5). Your agent never asks for your passwords, and you can remove its access at any time.
