# What you get

One project of your life (a career move, your money, your health) stops being scattered. Your AI agent builds it; you talk, choose and confirm.

![You talk to your agent, which sets up one spreadsheet in Google Drive. Your chat assistant keeps it current. The spreadsheet builds a private dashboard, a public page and a CV.](img/pieces.svg)

| | |
|---|---|
| **One spreadsheet** | In your own Google Drive. Every date, step and list. Edit it like any spreadsheet. |
| **A private dashboard** | Your whole project on one timeline, behind a login only you pass. |
| **A public page + CV** | Optional, from the same sheet, so they never go out of date. |
| **A chat assistant** | You say "today I sent the application"; it updates the sheet, the sites follow. |
