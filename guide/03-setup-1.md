# Setting it up: steps 1-4

Open your agent and paste the prompt from the cover. It leads from here, one question at a time.

## 1 Talk

**You:** Record yourself for 10-20 minutes about your goals, deadlines and documents, and send the text. Or answer its questions in the chat.

**Your agent:** Reads everything, then asks only about what is missing: dates, documents, people.

**Expect:** Questions like "when is that, roughly?". "Spring 2027" is a fine answer.

## 2 Goals

**You:** Read its goal list and correct it until it is right.

**Your agent:** Turns your words into 3-7 goals, each with its parts and the dates you know.

**Expect:** A short list in your words, plus a "not now" list and the questions still open.

## 3 Choose

**You:** Click through 3-4 versions of your dashboard, on your phone too, and say which feels closest.

**Your agent:** Builds real, clickable versions starting from Maya's setup, then refines the one you pick.

**Expect:** One or two rounds. Sample rows start with "Example:"; your real facts come next.

## 4 Store

**You:** Log in to Google once through a link it sends, then check the spreadsheet it made.

**Your agent:** Creates a folder in your Drive and fills the spreadsheet with facts you confirmed. Nothing else in your Drive is touched.

**Expect:** A sheet with tabs like Timelines, Steps and one tab per list. You can edit any cell.
