# Step 2 · Goals

A goal is something you want to have done by a certain time: "match into a residency program by 2027", "pay off the car loan by June". Your agent turns your talk into a short list of them. Everything it builds later is checked against this list.

**You:** Read the list. Correct anything wrong or missing, and say which goal matters most right now.

**Your agent:** Writes 3-7 goals in your words. Under each, the parts that lead to it and the dates you mentioned. Plus a "not now" list and the questions still open.

**Expect:** A message like the one below, then a few rounds of "change this" until you say yes.

> **Example (Maya):** **1. Match into a residency program**, applications open Sep 2027. Parts: applications, CV, CV boosters, research. **2. Graduate in May 2027.** Parts: rotations, assignments, exams, study. **3. Publish two papers** before applying. Parts: research. **4. Keep up with health check-ups**, titles only. Not now: moving plans, finances. Open question: the date of the Surgery II exam (ask the school).

## Why it matters

Each **part** becomes a lane on your dashboard's roadmap: one row per part of your project, across the months. Each **date** becomes a bar (a stretch of time), a diamond (a deadline) or a to-do.

> **Keep it short:** If the list grows past seven goals, your agent will ask which can wait. A dashboard with fifteen lanes gets ignored.

> **Other projects:** Money: "Car loan paid off by June 2027", "Three months of expenses saved", "Taxes filed by April". Health: "All check-ups done this year", "Run a 10K in the spring".
