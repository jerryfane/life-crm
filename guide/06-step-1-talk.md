# Step 1 · Talk

Open your AI agent and paste the prompt from the cover. Its first question is how you want to start. Everything begins with you describing your project in your own words.

**You:** Talk for 10-20 minutes about your project: what you want, by when, what is in the way, where your documents are. Then give your agent the text. If you prefer, answer its questions in the chat one at a time instead.

**Your agent:** Sends you a short list of things to talk about, reads everything you say, then asks only about what is missing.

**Expect:** Follow-up questions like "when is that, roughly?" or "who decides that?". "Spring 2027" is a fine answer.

## The easiest way: dictate

Talking is faster than typing, and it brings up things you would never write down: the letter you still have not asked for, the certificate buried in your email. A dictation app turns your voice into text in any app. **Wispr Flow** (wisprflow.ai, free plan available, Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android) works well: hold a key, talk, and the text appears in your agent's chat. Your phone's voice memos plus any transcription app work too.

## What to talk about

- What are you trying to achieve, and by when?
- What are the big parts of it?
- Which dates do you already know: deadlines, appointments, starts and ends?
- What is waiting on someone else right now?
- Where do your documents live today?
- Who else should see your dashboard, if anyone?

> **Example (Maya):** "I'm in my last year of med school, graduating May 2027. I want to match into residency, applications open in September. I need two papers published before that, I have one draft. I still need letters from three supervisors, Dr. Navarro said yes..."
