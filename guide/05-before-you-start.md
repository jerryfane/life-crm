# Before you start

| You need | Why |
|---|---|
| **An AI agent that can run commands** | Claude Code, Codex or similar, on your computer. It does all the technical work. A plain chat window is not enough for the setup. |
| **A Google account** | Your spreadsheet and documents live in your own Google Drive. |
| **About two hours** | Spread over a few days: one conversation, a few choices, two logins. You can stop after any step. |
| **A free Cloudflare account** | Optional, to put the websites online. Without it the dashboard still works as a file on your computer. |

| What it costs | |
|---|---|
| life-crm itself | Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) |
| Google Drive, Cloudflare | Free plans are enough |
| Your own web address | Optional, about 10-15 USD a year, and you buy it yourself |
| The AI agent | Whatever you already pay for it |

> **Have ready:** A list of what you want to keep track of (or just a voice memo about it), and the documents that matter: certificates, letters, statements, results. They can be scattered; sorting them is part of the work.

> **See it first:** life-crm.jerryfane.com shows a complete setup for Maya, a medical student. She is fictional: every name and date is made up. Click around before you start; your agent will use her setup as the starting point for yours.
