# Step 2: Goals

Your agent turns the conversation into a short list of goals, in your words. Everything it
builds later is checked against this list.

> **Your agent does:** writes 3-7 goals, each with the parts that lead to it and the dates you
> know, plus a "not now" list and the questions still open. It sends you the list and asks what
> is missing or wrong, and which goal matters most right now.

> **You do:** read it and correct it until it is right. Say yes when it is.

Maya's list looked like this:

> 1. **Match into a residency program**, applications open Sep 2027.
>    Parts: residency applications, CV, CV boosters, research.
> 2. **Graduate in May 2027.** Parts: rotations, assignments, exams, study.
> 3. **Publish two papers** before applying. Parts: research.
> 4. **Stay on top of health check-ups** (titles only).
>
> Not now: moving plans, finances.

Each **part** usually becomes a lane on your dashboard. If your list grows past seven goals,
your agent will ask which can wait: a dashboard with fifteen lanes gets ignored.

> **For money:** "Pay off the car loan by June 2027", "Three months of expenses saved",
> "Never miss a bill", "Taxes filed by April".

> **For health:** "All check-ups done this year", "Vitamin D back to normal", "Run a 10K in
> the spring".
