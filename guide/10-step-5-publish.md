# Step 5 · Publish

Your agent builds the websites from your spreadsheet and shows them to you on your computer. Then it puts them online for free on Cloudflare, a large hosting company, with a login in front of your dashboard.

**You:** Create a free Cloudflare account (or use yours) and log in once. Turn on the login with your agent guiding you, about 10 minutes the first time. Then read your public page line by line.

**Your agent:** Publishes the public page, and a locked page with no data for the dashboard. Once the login is on, it uploads your real dashboard and checks that a stranger sees only a login page.

**Expect:** You sign in with your email; Cloudflare emails you a one-time code. No password to remember.

## Addresses

You do not need to buy a domain. Every site gets a free address like **maya-crm.yourname.workers.dev**. If you already own a domain, your agent can use it; if you want to buy one (about 10-15 USD a year), it will ask first, and you buy it yourself.

## Why Cloudflare

It is the free option that can put a real login in front of a private website. Other free hosts can publish a site, but keeping it private needs a paid plan. The first time, Cloudflare asks you to set up "Zero Trust" (its name for the login service) and to pick the free plan. It may ask for a payment card even for the free plan; your agent tells you before, and you decide.

> **No Cloudflare?** The dashboard also works as a file on your computer. Open it in any browser, even offline. It just does not update itself.
