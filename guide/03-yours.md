# Yours to keep

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Only real facts** | Nothing is guessed. Unknown dates stay empty until you know. |
| **Your accounts** | Files in your Drive, sites on your Cloudflare. Nothing in anyone else's. |
| **Private stays private** | Health, money, phone, ID numbers never reach the public page. |
| **Ask first** | Nothing that costs money or shows something to others happens without your yes. |
| **Free to run** | Drive and Cloudflare free plans. A domain of your own is optional (about 10-15 USD a year). |

> **You need:** An AI agent that can run commands on a computer (Claude Code, Codex), a Google account, about two hours over a few days. Optional: a free Cloudflare account to put the sites online.

> **Not just careers:** A money CRM tracks debts, savings, bills and tax dates; a health CRM tracks check-ups, results and training. A move or a wedding works the same way: each part becomes a lane, each list a page.

> **See it first:** life-crm.jerryfane.com shows a complete setup for Maya, a medical student. She is fictional: every name and date is made up.

> **Get it:** github.com/jerryfane/life-crm, free and open source (AGPL-3.0).
