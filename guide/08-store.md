# Step 4: Store

Your data moves into your own Google Drive: one folder, subfolders for documents, and one
spreadsheet. From now on that spreadsheet is the single source of truth.

> **You do:** open a link your agent sends you and log in to Google (about 2 minutes). If you
> have never used Google Cloud, also open console.cloud.google.com once and accept the terms.
> Then check the spreadsheet your agent made and correct anything wrong. Move or send the
> documents that matter.

> **Your agent does:** connects to your Drive with a small tool called drivekey, creates the
> folder (for example "Maya CRM") and subfolders, fills the spreadsheet with your confirmed
> facts only, and walks you through its tabs.

What the login allows: your agent can read and change files in your Drive. It only works inside
the folder it creates, and you can switch it off any time at
myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## The spreadsheet

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Timelines | the lanes of your roadmap: name, color, goal |
| Steps | every period, deadline and task, with dates and status |
| Settings | the dashboard title and how many months it shows |
| Collections | which extra pages exist (one row per page) |
| one tab per page | the list itself: programs, bills, results, ... |
| Profile, Entries | only with a public page: your details and CV lines |

You can edit any cell yourself. Dates are written like `2027-05-14`, or just the month
(`2027-05`) if you do not know the day. Set `show` to `no` to hide a row without deleting it.

> **For money:** typical subfolders are Statements, Taxes, Contracts, Receipts. Never store
> passwords or full account numbers in the sheet.

> **For health:** typical subfolders are Results, Prescriptions, Insurance. Results stay
> private: they never reach a public page.
