# Step 1 · Talk

Open your AI agent and paste this prompt (it is also on the cover). Its first question is how you want to start.

{{prompt}}

**You:** Talk for 10-20 minutes about your project, then give your agent the text. Or answer its questions in the chat, one at a time.

**Your agent:** Sends you a short list of things to talk about, reads everything you say, then asks only about what is missing.

**Expect:** Follow-up questions like "when is that, roughly?" or "who decides that?". "Spring 2027" is a fine answer.

## The easiest way: dictate

Talking is faster than typing and brings up what you would never write down: the letter you have not asked for, the certificate buried in your email. **Wispr Flow** (wisprflow.ai, free plan, Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android) types what you say into any app, including your agent's chat.

## What to talk about

- What are you trying to achieve, by when, and what are its big parts?
- Which dates do you already know: deadlines, appointments, starts and ends?
- What is waiting on someone else, and where do your documents live?
- Who else should see your dashboard, if anyone?

> **Example (Maya):** "Last year of med school, I want to match into residency; applications open in September. I need two papers out by then, I have one draft..."
