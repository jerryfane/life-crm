# What life-crm is

Some projects have too many moving parts to keep in your head: applying to residency, getting out of debt, moving abroad. Dates live in emails, documents in five apps, to-dos in your head.

life-crm puts one project in one place, and an AI agent does the building. You talk, choose and confirm; you never write code.

![You talk to your chat assistant and to your agent. The agent builds a repo and one spreadsheet in Google Drive. The spreadsheet feeds a private dashboard and an optional public page, behind a Cloudflare login. Connectors like email and calendar bring in facts.](img/framework.svg)

> **What you end up with:** One spreadsheet in your Google Drive, the only place the truth lives. A private dashboard with your whole project on one timeline. Optionally a public page and a CV from the same sheet. And your everyday chat assistant keeping it all current.
