# What life-crm is

Some projects have too many moving parts to keep in your head: applying to residency, getting out of debt, moving abroad, staying on top of your health. Dates live in emails, documents in five apps, to-dos in your head, and something always slips.

A CRM is what companies use to keep track of every customer, deal and next step. life-crm is the same idea for one project of your life: one place that knows every date, document and next step, and shows it back to you clearly.

You do not build it yourself. You paste one prompt into an AI agent, and it interviews you, proposes a setup, and builds it with you. You talk, choose and confirm; you never write code.

| | |
|---|---|
| **One spreadsheet** | In your own Google Drive. It holds every date, step and list: the only place the truth lives. You can open and edit it like any spreadsheet. |
| **A private dashboard** | A website only you can log into. Your whole project on one timeline, countdowns to what is next, and pages for the lists you compare. |
| **A public page and CV** | Optional. A one-page portfolio and a PDF CV made from the same sheet, so they never go out of date. |
| **A chat assistant that keeps it current** | The AI you already chat with on your phone. "Today I sent the application" updates the sheet, and the websites follow. |

> **Meet Maya:** This guide follows Maya, a medical student applying to residency. She is fictional: every name and date is made up. Her complete setup is live at life-crm.jerryfane.com, and your agent uses it as the starting point for yours.
