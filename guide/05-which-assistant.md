# Which chat assistant?

To update your sheet, an assistant must be able to edit your Google Drive, not only read it. Checked in October 2026; apps change fast, so your agent checks again in step 6.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Claude** · claude.ai | Chat in the Claude app, on the web or on desktop. **Updates your sheet:** yes, in beta, from web and desktop. **Setup:** easy, switch on its Google connectors. |
| **Muse** by Meta · ai.meta.com/muse | Chat in **WhatsApp** or the Muse app. **Updates your sheet:** it connects to Google, but Meta has not yet confirmed Sheets editing. **Setup:** easy, tap Connectors. US only for now; free with a usage limit. |
| **Hermes Agent** by Nous Research · hermes-agent.nousresearch.com | Chat in **Telegram**, **WhatsApp**, Slack and more. **Updates your sheet:** yes, it reads and writes Sheets. **Setup:** technical, so your agent installs it and sets up Google access. Free software; you pay for the AI it runs on. |
| **OpenClaw** · openclaw.ai | Chat in **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, iMessage and more. **Updates your sheet:** yes, it reads and writes Sheets. **Setup:** technical, the same way. It runs on your own computer, which must stay on. |
| **Grok Bot** by xAI · docs.x.ai/grok-bot | Chat in its own app, not WhatsApp or Telegram. **Updates your sheet:** it has a Google Sheets connector; editing is not documented. **Setup:** easy, but paid, from $20 a month. |

> **Simplest start:** Claude, if you already use it. **Want it in WhatsApp or Telegram?** Muse if you are in the US; otherwise Hermes Agent or OpenClaw, which your agent can install for you.

## Not on the list

- **Dot** (New Computer) closed in October 2025.
- The **Gemini** app can read your Drive but not change it.
- The **Grok** chatbot inside Telegram has no access to your Drive.
- **ChatGPT for Google Sheets** lives inside the sheet, so it is not a chat assistant.
