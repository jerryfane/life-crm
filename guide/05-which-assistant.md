# Which chat assistant?

An assistant must be able to edit your Google Drive, not only read it. Checked in October 2026; your agent checks again in step 6.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Claude** · claude.ai | Chat in the Claude app, web or desktop. **Updates your sheet:** yes, in beta, from web and desktop. **Setup:** easy, switch on its Google connectors. |
| **dots** by OpenAI · learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots | Chat in ChatGPT (start on desktop), Slack or Teams; texting is coming. **Updates your sheet:** yes, with the Google Drive plugin. **Setup:** easy, but needs ChatGPT Pro (from $100 a month); not in the EU, UK or Switzerland. |
| **Muse** by Meta · ai.meta.com/muse | Chat in **WhatsApp** or the Muse app. **Updates your sheet:** connects to Google; Meta has not confirmed Sheets editing yet. **Setup:** easy, tap Connectors. US only; free with a limit. |
| **Hermes Agent** by Nous Research · hermes-agent.nousresearch.com | Chat in **Telegram**, **WhatsApp**, Slack and more. **Updates your sheet:** yes. **Setup:** technical, so your agent installs it and sets up Google access. Free software; you pay for the AI it runs on. |
| **OpenClaw** · openclaw.ai | Chat in **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, iMessage and more. **Updates your sheet:** yes. **Setup:** technical, the same way. Runs on your own computer, which must stay on. |
| **Grok Bot** by xAI · docs.x.ai/grok-bot | Chat in its own app only. **Updates your sheet:** has a Sheets connector; editing not documented. **Setup:** easy; paid, from $20 a month. |

> **Simplest start:** Claude, if you already use it. **Want it in WhatsApp or Telegram?** Muse if you are in the US; otherwise Hermes Agent or OpenClaw, which your agent can install for you.

> **Skip:** The Gemini app (reads Drive, cannot edit), the Grok chatbot in Telegram (no Drive access), ChatGPT for Google Sheets (lives inside the sheet).
