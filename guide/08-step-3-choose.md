# Step 3 · Choose

Now you decide what your dashboard shows and how it looks. You do not design anything: your agent proposes, you pick.

**You:** Answer a few quick multiple-choice questions, then click through the versions it sends and say which feels closest and what you would change.

**Your agent:** Starts from Maya's setup and adapts it to your goals on its own. It builds 3-4 real, working versions of your dashboard and one page with buttons to switch between them, then sends you the link (or screenshots).

**Expect:** One or two rounds. The versions show your confirmed facts; anything else is a sample row starting with "Example:".

## What you choose

| Choice | What it means | Example question |
|---|---|---|
| **Lanes** | The rows of your roadmap, one per part of your project. | "Maya has 7 lanes. For you I'd make these 5. Add, merge or drop any?" |
| **Lists** | Things you compare get their own page, as a table or as cards. | "Should the apartments you are looking at be a page? Table or cards?" |
| **Time span** | How many months the roadmap shows at once. | "12 months for the overview, or 6 for more detail?" |
| **Public page** | Whether you also want a portfolio page and a CV. | "Only the private dashboard, or a public page too?" |
| **Who sees it** | Only you, or also a partner, parent or coach. | "Should anyone else be able to log in?" |

> **Look on your phone too:** If you will mostly check it on your phone, open the versions there before you pick. Everything works on a phone.
