# Step 1: Talk

Everything starts with a conversation about your project. Talking is faster than typing, and it
brings up things you would never write down: the letter you still have not asked for, the
certificate somewhere in your email.

> **You do:** record yourself for 10-20 minutes, alone or with someone who knows your plans.
> Turn it into text with any voice-to-text tool (your phone's voice memos, a transcription app,
> or your chat assistant) and give the text to your agent. Or, if you prefer, answer its
> questions in the chat, one at a time.

> **Your agent does:** sends you a short list of questions to talk through, reads the whole
> transcript, and then asks only about what is missing: dates, documents, people.

Questions it will ask you about, in some form:

- What are you trying to achieve, and by when?
- What are the big parts of it?
- Which dates do you already know?
- What is waiting on someone else?
- Where do your documents live today?
- Who else should see the dashboard, if anyone?

You do not need exact answers. "Spring 2027" is fine; it shows as approximate on the dashboard
until you know more.

> **For money:** talk about debts and their rates, savings goals, bills and when they come, tax
> dates, contracts that renew. You decide whether amounts go in at all.

> **For health:** talk about check-ups that are due, results you want to follow, a habit or a
> race you are training for. You decide how much detail is stored: titles only ("Dentist") is a
> fine choice. Health never appears on a public page.
