# Privacy and honesty

These rules are built into the agent skill. Knowing them helps you notice if something goes
wrong.

| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Only real facts** | Your agent and assistant never fill gaps with guesses. Unknown stays empty until you know. |
| **Your accounts** | Your files live in your Drive, your sites in your Cloudflare account. Nothing goes into someone else's. |
| **Private stays private** | Health, money, phone, address, ID numbers and other people's details never go on the public page. |
| **Dashboard behind a login** | Your data is only uploaded once the login is on, and every update checks it again. |
| **Ask first** | Nothing that costs money, creates an account or shows something to others happens without your yes. |
| **You can leave** | Your spreadsheet works without any of this. Remove access any time (step 7). |

## Questions

**Can someone find my dashboard?** They can find the address, but only the emails you allowed
get past the login.

**Is my data used to train AI?** That depends on the AI agent and chat assistant you use, not
on this kit. Check their settings.

**What if the agent makes a mistake in my sheet?** Google Sheets keeps a version history
(File → Version history), so any change can be undone.

**Can I use this for two projects?** Yes: one folder and spreadsheet per project, or several
lanes in one. Ask your agent which fits.

**I am a developer.** The apps, the spreadsheet format and the skill are documented in the
repository: github.com/jerryfane/life-crm.
