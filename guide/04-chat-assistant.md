# Your personal chat assistant

Your agent builds things; your chat assistant keeps them current. A personal chat assistant is an AI you message the way you message a friend: in WhatsApp, Telegram or its own app, on your phone or laptop. Once it can open your Google Drive, it reads your spreadsheet to answer questions, and edits it when you tell it what happened. You never have to open the sheet yourself.

## How it knows your project

Your agent writes a short guide file into your Drive folder (step 6). It tells your assistant which tab is which, what it may change on its own, and what needs your yes first. Your assistant reads it before answering anything about your project.

## What a day looks like

> **You:** Today I submitted the Riverside application, and Dr. Navarro agreed to write my letter.

> **Assistant:** Done: Riverside is now "applied" in Programs, and "Letter from Dr. Navarro" is marked done in Steps. Your next deadline is the Lakeshore application on 1 November.

> **You:** What do I still need for Lakeshore?

> **Assistant:** From your sheet: a personal statement (not started) and your exam score report (status "to book").

## Good to know

- It answers from your sheet, not from memory, so the answers are only as good as the sheet. Tell it when things happen.
- Some assistants can read Drive but not edit it. They can still answer, and tell you exactly what to change.
- Your agent sets it up with you and tests it with two messages before you rely on it. The next page compares the assistants you can use.
