# Privacy, rules and questions

| Rule | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| **Only real facts** | Your agent and assistant never fill gaps with guesses. Unknown stays empty. |
| **Your accounts** | Files in your Drive, sites in your Cloudflare. Nothing goes into anyone else's. |
| **Private stays private** | Health, money, phone, address, ID numbers never reach the public page. Your phone number is only in your own copy of the CV. |
| **Behind a login** | Your dashboard data is uploaded only once the login works, and every update checks it again. |
| **Ask first** | Nothing that costs money, creates an account or shows something to others happens without your yes. |

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Not a career project?** | Money (debts, savings, bills, taxes), health (check-ups, results, training), a move, a wedding: each part becomes a lane, each list a page. |
| **Can someone find my dashboard?** | They may find the address, but only the emails you allowed get past the login. |
| **Is my data used to train AI?** | That depends on the agent and assistant you use, not on life-crm. Check their settings. |
| **Can I stop using it?** | Yes. The spreadsheet is yours and works on its own. |
