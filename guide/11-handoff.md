# Step 7: Handoff

Your agent hands everything over, so you can use your CRM without it.

> **Your agent does:** writes a one-page *MY-CRM* note in your folder with every link (sheet,
> dashboard, public page, CV, folder) and three short "how to" sections. It shows you the three
> things below on your phone, explains what access it still has and how to remove it, and asks
> what is missing or too much.

> **You do:** follow along on your phone, and say whether the agent should keep its access for
> upkeep.

## On your phone

1. Open your dashboard, log in, and add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen on
   iPhone; menu → Add to home screen on Android).
2. Tell your assistant something you did today.
3. Watch the change appear on the dashboard after the update delay.

## Removing access, any time

| Access | How to remove it |
|---|---|
| Your agent's Google Drive login | myaccount.google.com/permissions → "Google Cloud SDK" → remove |
| Your agent's Cloudflare access | delete the API token, if you created one |
| Your assistant's Drive access | the assistant's connector settings |

Your spreadsheet, documents and websites stay yours whatever you remove.
