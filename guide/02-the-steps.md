# How it goes

Paste the prompt from the cover into your agent. From then on it leads, one question at a time.

| Step | You | Your agent |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Talk** | Record 10-20 minutes about your goals, deadlines and documents. | Reads it and asks only what is missing. |
| **2 Goals** | Correct its short goal list. | Turns your words into 3-7 goals. |
| **3 Choose** | Click through 3-4 versions of your dashboard; pick one. | Builds real, clickable options. |
| **4 Store** | Log in to Google once; check the sheet. | Creates your Drive folder and spreadsheet, with confirmed facts only. |
| **5 Publish** | Turn on the login with its help (10 minutes). | Puts the sites online; your data goes up only behind the login. |
| **6 Assistant** | Connect your chat assistant to Drive. | Writes the guide it follows. |
| **7 Handoff** | Open it on your phone. | Shows you every link and how to remove its access. |
| **8 Keep going** | Tell your assistant what you did. | Sets up automatic updates. |

You can stop after any step and continue another day.
