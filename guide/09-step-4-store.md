# Step 4 · Store

Your data moves into your own Google Drive: one folder, subfolders for documents, and one spreadsheet. From now on that spreadsheet is the single source of truth.

**You:** Open a link your agent sends and log in to Google (about 2 minutes). If you have never used Google Cloud, also open console.cloud.google.com once and accept the terms. Then check the spreadsheet and correct anything wrong.

**Your agent:** Connects to your Drive with a small tool called drivekey, creates the folder and subfolders, fills the spreadsheet with your confirmed facts only, and walks you through its tabs.

**Expect:** A folder like "Maya CRM" in your Drive. Nothing outside it is touched. You can switch the access off any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Your spreadsheet

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Timelines** | The lanes of your roadmap: name, color, goal. |
| **Steps** | Every stretch of time, deadline and to-do, with dates and status (to do, doing, done). |
| **Collections** | Which extra pages exist, one row per page. |
| **One tab per page** | The list itself: programs, papers, bills, results. |
| **Settings** | The dashboard title and how many months it shows. |
| **Profile, Entries** | Only with a public page: your details, and one row per line of your CV. |

> **Editing it yourself:** Any cell can be changed by hand. Dates are written like 2027-05-14, or just the month (2027-05) if you do not know the day. Set "show" to "no" to hide a row without deleting it.
