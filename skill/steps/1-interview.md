# Step 1: Interview

**Goal:** understand the project in the person's own words: what they want to achieve, by when,
what is scattered, and who else is involved.

## What they do

Talk. The best start is a **recorded conversation**: they record themselves (or a talk with a
friend or partner) for 10-20 minutes about the project, then turn it into text with any
voice-to-text tool (their phone's voice memos, a transcription app, or their chat assistant)
and paste the text or send the file. Talking is faster than typing and brings up things nobody
would write down.

If they prefer, interview them live in the chat instead.

## What you do

1. Offer the two ways to start, as a choice:
   - **Record and send a transcript** (recommended: richer, and they can do it on a walk)
   - **Answer questions here, one at a time**
2. Ask what kind of project it is (career, money, health, a move, a wedding, studies, other). If
   it mixes several, that is fine: each becomes its own timeline later.
3. Pick the matching questions from [../templates/interview.md](../templates/interview.md).
   - Transcript route: send the 6-8 questions as a list they can talk through.
   - Live route: ask them **one at a time**, and follow up on anything vague ("when is that,
     roughly?", "who decides that?").
4. When the transcript arrives, read it fully. Then list what is still missing (dates,
   documents, people) and ask only about those.
5. Ask where their documents live today (email, Drive, phone photos, a drawer) and which ones
   matter: certificates, letters, contracts, results, statements.
6. Save the transcript as `work/interview.md` and start `work/NOTES.md`.

## Good to know

- People rarely know every date. "Spring 2027" is a fine answer; you will store it as a month
  and the dashboard shows it as approximate.
- Listen for **deadlines** (hard dates with consequences), **waiting** (something depends on
  someone else) and **repeats** (every month, every year). Each becomes a different kind of row
  later.
- Listen for other people: a partner who helps, a supervisor, a doctor, an accountant. Ask
  whether anyone else should see the dashboard.
- Do not design anything yet. If they ask "what will it look like?", show them Maya's demo at
  https://life-crm.jerryfane.com and say you will tailor it after the next step.

## Done when

You have their own words for: the goal(s), the time frame, the main parts of the project, the
key dates they know, where documents are, and who is involved. `work/interview.md` exists.
