# Step 2: Goals

**Goal:** turn the conversation into a short goal list the person agrees with. Everything you
build later is measured against it.

## What you do

1. Write `work/GOALS.md` using [../templates/goals.md](../templates/goals.md):
   - **3-7 goals**, each one line, in their words, with a target date if they gave one.
   - Under each goal: the parts that lead to it (these become **timelines** in step 3), and the
     known dates (these become **steps**).
   - A short **not now** list: things they mentioned but do not want to track yet.
   - **Open questions**: facts you still need, each with who can answer it.
2. Send them the list, short, and ask:
   - "Is anything missing or wrong?"
   - "Which goal matters most right now?" (it goes first on the dashboard)
3. Fix it until they say yes. Record the confirmation in `work/NOTES.md` with the date.

## Example (Maya)

> 1. **Match into a residency program**, applications open Sep 2027.
>    Parts: residency applications, CV, CV boosters (electives, certificates), research.
> 2. **Graduate in May 2027.** Parts: rotations, assignments, exams, study.
> 3. **Publish two papers** before applying. Parts: research.
> 4. **Stay on top of health check-ups** (titles only, no details).
>
> Not now: moving plans, finances.

## Good to know

- One goal can need several timelines, and one timeline can serve several goals. That is fine.
- If they list more than 7 goals, ask which can wait. A dashboard with 15 lanes gets ignored.
- Goals with no date are still goals. Ask "by when would you like this?" once; if they do not
  know, leave it.

## Done when

They have confirmed `work/GOALS.md`.
