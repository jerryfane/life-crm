# Interview questions

Pick 6-8. For a recorded talk, send them as a list to talk through. Live, ask one at a time and
follow up on anything vague.

## For every project

1. What are you trying to achieve, and by when?
2. What does "done" or "it worked" look like for you?
3. What are the big parts of it? (Each part usually becomes a lane on the roadmap.)
4. Which dates do you already know: deadlines, appointments, starts and ends?
5. What is waiting on someone else right now? Who?
6. What keeps slipping or getting forgotten?
7. Where do the documents live today: email, Drive, phone photos, paper?
8. Who else is involved or should see the dashboard?
9. What would you check on your phone once a day?
10. What must stay private?

## Career (studies, job search, applications)

- Which programs, schools or employers are on your list? What do you compare them on?
- What is on your CV now, and what is missing for the next application?
- Which letters, certificates or exams do you need, and from whom?
- Any research, papers or projects with a target date?
- Do you want a public page and a CV made from the same data?

## Money

- Which debts, savings goals or big purchases are you working toward? Target amounts and dates?
- Which bills come every month or year, and when?
- Tax dates, contracts that renew, subscriptions to cancel?
- Do you want amounts in the dashboard, or only dates and status? (It is private behind a
  login, but some people prefer no numbers.)

## Health

- Which check-ups or appointments are due or overdue?
- Any results you want to follow over time?
- A habit or training goal with a date (a race, a weight, sleep)?
- How much detail do you want stored: titles only ("Dentist"), or results too? (Never public.)

## A move, a wedding, an event

- What is the date, and what has to happen before it?
- Which lists are you juggling (venues, apartments, suppliers, guests)? What do you compare?
- Which bookings or paperwork have deadlines?
- Who is helping, and who decides?
