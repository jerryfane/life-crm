You talk. Your agent builds.

It asks one thing at a time, shows you options to pick from, and explains anything you need to click.

  1. 30 min

    Talk

    Your goals, deadlines and papers, in your own words.

  2. 20–30 min

    Choose

    Click through a few designs, starting from Maya’s.

  3. 15 min

    Store

    One spreadsheet in your Drive. Edit it any time.

  4. 15 min

    Publish

    Your dashboard goes online, behind a login.

  5. 20 min

    Hand off

    Your chat assistant learns your sheet. Then you just tell it what happened.

About two hours in total, over a few days. You can stop after any step.

Private by design.

Your data stays in your own accounts, in a spreadsheet you can read. Nothing is locked in.

  • Behind a login.Only the emails you allow can open your dashboard.
  • In your Drive.Your sheet, your folders. Nothing in anyone else’s account.
  • Off the public page.Health, money, phone number and address never appear on it.
  • Only real facts.Your agent never guesses. What you don’t know stays empty.
