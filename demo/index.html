How it works

You talk; your agent does the building. You only need an AI agent that can run commands, a Google account and a free Cloudflare account.

  1. TalkRecord yourself describing your goals, deadlines and the documents scattered around.
  2. ChooseYour agent asks questions and shows clickable options, starting from Maya's setup.
  3. StoreYour data goes into Google Drive: folders and a spreadsheet you can edit any time.
  4. BuildIt builds your dashboard, public page and CV, and puts them online.
  5. Keep goingTell a chat assistant "today I did X". The sheet updates; the sites follow.

Yours to keep

Your data stays readableA spreadsheet in your own Drive. No app to log into, nothing to export, nothing locked in.
Private by defaultThe dashboard sits behind a login only you (and whoever you choose) can pass. Your phone number never reaches the public page.
