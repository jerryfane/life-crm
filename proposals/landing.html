A kit for your AI agent

Your life project, kept in one place.

Applying to residency, paying off a loan, training for a race: the dates, steps and papers end up in ten places. Paste one prompt into your AI agent. It interviews you, then builds a private dashboard from one Google Sheet that stays in your own Drive.

Paste this into Claude Code or Codex

I want a personal CRM for a project in my life. Download https://github.com/jerryfane/life-crm (git clone, or the ZIP), read skill/SKILL.md, and follow it step by step with me. I am not a developer: ask me one thing at a time, show me options to choose from, and explain anything I need to click. Start with step 1.

§ 1

How it works

You talk and choose; your agent does the building. About two hours in total, spread over a few days. You can stop after any step and pick it up another day.

  1. 01

    Talk

    Describe your project in your own words: goals, deadlines, the papers scattered around.

    30 minutes
  2. 02

    Choose

    Your agent shows a few clickable designs, starting from Maya’s. You pick the one that feels right.

    20–30 minutes
  3. 03

    Store

    Everything goes into one spreadsheet in your Google Drive. Open and edit it like any sheet.

    15 minutes
  4. 04

    Publish

    Your dashboard goes online behind a login. A public page and a CV, if you want them.

    15 minutes
  5. 05

    Keep it current

    Tell your everyday chat assistant what you did. It updates the sheet; the dashboard follows.

    A minute a day

After setup, you just tell it what happened.

No forms, no app to open. Your chat assistant reads a short guide your agent writes for it, so it knows your sheet and keeps it tidy.

    For example Claude Code or Codex. A plain chat window is not enough for the setup; it is fine afterwards.

  2. ii.
    A Google account

  3. iii.
    About two hours

§ 4

Yours, and private

These rules are built into what your agent follows. Knowing them helps you notice if something goes wrong.

Your sheet stays in your Drive. Your dashboard stays behind your login. Nothing goes into anyone else’s account.
  • Private stays private

    Health, money, phone number and address never go on the public page. Only the emails you allow get past the login.

  • Only real facts

    Your agent never fills gaps with guesses. What you don’t know yet stays empty until you do.

  • You can always leave

    Your spreadsheet works without any of this. Google keeps a version history, so any change can be undone.
